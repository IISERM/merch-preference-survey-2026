Survey Summary & Key Findings

The survey was conducted to understand community preferences for campus merchandise. The findings assist clubs in creating apparel that people want to wear while managing production costs.

Main Findings

  • Design and Quality Matter Most: 91% of respondents judge merchandise by its design, fabric quality, and comfort rather than which club releases it. Most respondents evaluate each item individually.
  • Back Print Preference: 84% of respondents prefer the main graphic or logo to be placed on the back of clothing. Front prints should remain small and subtle.
  • Oversized Fit Demand: Over half of text feedback requested relaxed, baggy, or oversized fits instead of standard tight cuts.
  • T-shirt/Hoodie Pricing: 82% of respondents are willing to pay up to ₹800 for a sweatshirt or T-shirt/hoodie. Pricing above ₹800 significantly reduces potential buyers.

Recommendations for Clubs & Committees

1. Material & Silhouette Use 100% Cotton (240+ GSM for T-shirts, 300+ GSM for T-shirt/hoodies). Offer oversized, drop-shoulder, or boxy fit options.
2. Print Placement Place main artwork on the back of clothing. Keep the front minimal with a small chest print or logo.
3. Pricing Guidelines T-Shirts: ₹350 – ₹500
T-shirt/Hoodies & Sweatshirts: ₹550 – ₹750

High-Demand Niche Merchandise

  • Penguin Plushies: High interest in Linux mascot plushies and soft desk toys.
  • Metal Keychains: Durable keychains with hostel or club identifiers.
  • Desk Accessories: Custom mousepads, pen holders, and field notebooks.
  • Lab Apparel: Science-themed lab coats and aprons.
