# Merch Preferences 2026 (IISERM)

This repository hosts and provides the data collected in the survey asking for the community's preferences and opinions about the merch they will be willing to buy.
The goal of this initiative is to eliminate guesswork, reduce financial risk for clubs, and ensure the student body receives functional, beautifully-designed items they actually want to wear and use.

The data of the survey is in data/iiserm_merch_survey_data.csv
We encourage the community and other members to draw inferences from the data and share those with the student body to help the concerned bodies to design relevant merchandise.

---

## Quick Insights

### 1. Top Merchandise Demands
When forced to choose **only ONE item** to purchase next semester, the campus community prioritized:
* **T-Shirts:** 33.3%
* **Hoodies & Sweatshirts:** 24.1%
* **Laptop Bags, Sleeves & Backpacks:** 16.7%
* **Full-Zip Jackets:** 11.1%

### 2. Design & Placement Guidelines

| Design Element | Preference Level | Actionable Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Front Placement** | Minimalist/Small Design | Keep the front clean. Massive "Hero" designs on the front are heavily disliked. |
| **Back Placement** | Large/Hero Design | Put the primary artwork, typography, or graphic elements on the back. |
| **Sleeves** | Not Preferred | Avoid sleeve printing altogether to save costs and keep layouts clean. |
| **Color Scheme** | Dark Themes | Over 74% of respondents prefer Dark Palettes (Black, Navy Blue, Charcoal). |
| **Branding** | Insti logo or text  | Over 81% demand the clear inclusion of **'IISER Mohali'** text or logos. |

### 3. Economics & Budget Constraints
For high-value items like Hoodies, Sweatshirts, and Jackets:
* **400 – 800 INR** represents the ideal pricing corridor.
* Pricing items above **800 INR** reduces potential buyer base significantly.
